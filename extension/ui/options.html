Viral Video Agent

Keys stay in this browser only (chrome.storage.local). Nothing is sent anywhere except Google and Telegram.

1. Gemini

Google gates some models per account. If Test returns a 404, press “List models” and pick one your key can actually call.

2. Telegram

Use a different bot from the one your Render bot in index.js polls. Two programs polling one token make Telegram answer 409 and messages go missing.

Send any message to your bot first, then press “Find it”. Or send /start — the bot replies with your id.

3. Pipeline

Turn off “Ask where to save each file” in chrome://settings/downloads or every image will prompt you.

4. Artistly loop timing

The delay is randomised between min and max so the site does not see a metronome.

5. Site selectors

The dashboards are behind a login, so the agent guesses the prompt box and the Generate button. If it guesses wrong, open the site, press “Teach selector” in the panel and click the box and the button — they are filled in here automatically and always win over the guesses.
